Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse our products, click "Add to Cart" on what you want, then go to your cart and fill in your name, phone number, and delivery address. Submit the form and we will contact you to confirm.

How long does delivery take?

Orders within Benin City are usually delivered the same day or within 24 hours after confirmation.

How much is delivery?

Delivery fees depend on your location. We will tell you the exact fee when we confirm your order.

How can I pay?

You can pay by bank transfer or pay on delivery.

Bank transfer details:

Bank: GTBank
Account number: 0123456789
Account name: Your Name or Business Name

After paying, send us your payment receipt on WhatsApp so we can confirm your order.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, as long as it has not been sent out for delivery. Contact us as soon as possible.
